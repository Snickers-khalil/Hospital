Live Demo Guide

Multi-Hospital Demo Walkthrough

This page is a standalone presenter guide for showing the system live. It follows a simple patient journey from login to clinical action and operational follow-up.

Live app preview

Use the local app preview while presenting. The URL below points to the running project so the walkthrough stays grounded in the real system.

Open the live app

1. Start the demo

  1. Open the login page and use a demo account such as admin@hms.com or doctor@hms.com.
  2. Use password 12345.
  3. Show the dashboard and explain that the menu changes based on the role.

2. Patient workflow

  1. Open the Patient module.
  2. Create or review an existing patient record.
  3. Show personal details, contact information, and case history.

3. Appointment flow

  1. Open the Appointment module.
  2. Select a patient and doctor.
  3. Choose a date and time, then save the appointment.
  4. Show the appointment list and status.

4. Doctor clinical view

  1. Switch to the Doctor role.
  2. Open the patient case or appointment.
  3. Show diagnosis, prescription, and treatment notes.
  4. Explain how the doctor can continue the case from one screen to the next.

5. Operational modules

  • Show pharmacy or medicine inventory if you want to discuss dispensing.
  • Show finance or billing to explain payment tracking.
  • Optionally open lab/radiology or reports for more context.

6. Presentation tips

  • Keep the story centered on one patient journey.
  • Switch roles quickly to show role-based access.
  • Emphasize that the platform is modular and configurable.
